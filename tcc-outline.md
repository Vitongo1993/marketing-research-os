# Core Research Argument

The central argument of this TCC is that reducing friction in the initial customer journey, combined with CRM automation and decentralized lead ownership, was associated with improvements in the commercial funnel of Telex Soluções Auditivas.

The case suggests that customer journey optimization is not only a communication challenge, but an operational architecture challenge involving marketing, CRM, automation, sales and service delivery.

---

# Main Research Question

How did the redesign of the customer journey, CRM flow and lead management process affect lead generation, appointment scheduling, attendance and sales conversion at Telex Soluções Auditivas?

---

# General Objective

Analyze how the simplification of the customer journey, combined with CRM automation and decentralized lead ownership, affected the commercial funnel indicators of Telex Soluções Auditivas.

---

# Specific Objectives

1. Map the original 2024 customer journey and identify the main friction points.

2. Describe the redesigned 2025 journey, including chatbot entry, CRM integration, automation and store-level ownership.

3. Compare funnel indicators before and after the redesign.

4. Analyze how response time and scheduling time changed after the intervention.

5. Evaluate the operational implications of transferring lead ownership from the call center to stores.

6. Discuss the trade-off between increased lead volume and increased ghost or low-quality leads.

7. Identify managerial and academic implications of the case.

---
