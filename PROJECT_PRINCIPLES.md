# Project Principles

## Purpose

The Marketing Research OS is built upon a set of non-negotiable principles that guide every architectural decision, document, framework, prompt, and contribution.

These principles ensure that the project remains scientifically rigorous, ethically responsible, practically useful, and continuously evolving.

---

# Core Principles

## 1. Scientific Rigor

Scientific evidence is the foundation of every recommendation.

All concepts, models, and conclusions should be supported by reliable and verifiable academic sources whenever possible.

Claims without evidence must be explicitly identified as hypotheses, interpretations, or opinions.

---

## 2. Critical Thinking

Artificial Intelligence must encourage critical analysis rather than passive acceptance.

The project should challenge assumptions, explore alternative explanations, identify limitations, and acknowledge uncertainty.

The objective is not to confirm ideas, but to improve them.

---

## 3. Transparency

Every recommendation should make its assumptions explicit.

Whenever uncertainty exists, it should be communicated clearly.

Readers must always be able to distinguish between:

- established evidence;
- interpretation;
- recommendation;
- hypothesis;
- opinion.

---

## 4. Human Oversight

Artificial Intelligence supports research.

Human judgment remains responsible for scientific decisions, ethical considerations, and final conclusions.

AI augments researchers.

It never replaces them.

---

## 5. Evidence First

Whenever multiple sources disagree, preference should be given to higher-quality evidence.

The recommended order is:

1. Peer-reviewed scientific literature
2. Academic books
3. Systematic reviews
4. Meta-analyses
5. Official reports
6. Professional publications
7. Specialized blogs (only as complementary references)

---

## 6. Reproducibility

Research methods should be documented in sufficient detail to allow replication whenever possible.

The framework encourages transparent workflows, explicit methodologies, and traceable decisions.

---

## 7. Modularity

Knowledge should be organized into independent modules.

Every document must have a single clear purpose and should remain useful even when read independently.

---

## 8. Continuous Improvement

Knowledge evolves.

The Marketing Research OS is a living framework.

Every document should be periodically reviewed, updated, and improved as new evidence becomes available.

---

## 9. Practical Relevance

Scientific knowledge should generate practical value.

Whenever appropriate, theoretical concepts should be connected to real-world business applications and organizational decision-making.

---

## 10. Ethical Use of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence must be used responsibly.

The project rejects:

- fabricated references;
- plagiarism;
- manipulation of scientific evidence;
- misleading interpretations;
- hidden limitations.

Ethics is not optional.

It is a design requirement.

---

# Decision Filter

Whenever a new document, framework, prompt, or contribution is proposed, ask the following questions:

- Does it improve scientific rigor?
- Is it supported by reliable evidence?
- Is it transparent?
- Is it ethically responsible?
- Can it be maintained over time?
- Does it create practical value?
- Does it respect the modular architecture of the project?

If the answer to any of these questions is "No", the proposal should be reviewed before being incorporated into the repository.

---

# Guiding Philosophy

The objective of the Marketing Research OS is not to replace researchers.

Its objective is to help researchers think more critically, organize knowledge more effectively, and produce higher-quality scientific work with the support of Artificial Intelligence.

The framework values curiosity, intellectual honesty, scientific rigor, continuous learning, and responsible innovation above speed or convenience.
