# Marketing Research OS (MROS)

> **An Open Knowledge Framework for AI-Assisted Marketing Research**
>
> *Building a structured, evidence-based and AI-assisted ecosystem for scientific research in Marketing.*

---

## Overview

The **Marketing Research OS (MROS)** is an open knowledge framework designed to support researchers, graduate students, professionals and organizations in conducting high-quality scientific research in Marketing with the assistance of Artificial Intelligence.

Rather than being a traditional manual, MROS is a modular knowledge system that integrates academic theory, practical frameworks, prompt engineering, research methodologies and real-world case studies into a continuously evolving repository.

The framework combines scientific rigor with modern AI capabilities while maintaining transparency, reproducibility and critical thinking as fundamental principles.

---

## Why MROS?

Scientific research is becoming increasingly interdisciplinary and AI-assisted. However, researchers often face challenges such as:

- Fragmented theoretical references
- Difficulty organizing literature
- Poor integration between AI tools and research methods
- Lack of standardized research workflows
- Scattered prompts with no methodological foundation
- Limited connection between academic theory and business practice

MROS addresses these challenges by providing a structured and reusable knowledge framework.

# Case Study

The first practical application of the Marketing Research OS is the redesign of the customer journey implemented at **Telex Soluções Auditivas**.

This case study explores how reducing friction in the lead acquisition process, redesigning the customer journey and decentralizing scheduling operations contributed to improvements in lead generation, appointment scheduling and sales performance.

The case will serve as the empirical foundation for the MBA Final Project (TCC) in Marketing Management at UFRJ.
