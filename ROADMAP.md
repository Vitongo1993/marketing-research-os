# Purpose

The purpose of this roadmap is to define the long-term evolution of the Marketing Research OS (MROS), ensuring that the repository grows in a structured, modular and sustainable manner.

The roadmap serves as a strategic guide for planning, prioritizing and monitoring the development of the project.

---

# Guiding Principles

The roadmap follows the governance established by:

- ADR-0001
- PROJECT_PRINCIPLES.md
- PROJECT_CHARTER.md

Every planned module must respect the project's architectural and scientific principles.

---

# Development Strategy

The project will evolve through incremental releases.

Each version must:

- Deliver practical value.
- Maintain documentation quality.
- Preserve modularity.
- Improve scientific rigor.
- Expand the knowledge base without compromising consistency.

The Marketing Research OS is intended to become an open knowledge framework capable of supporting:

- MBA research
- Master's research
- Doctoral research
- Scientific publications
- Professional consulting projects
- AI-assisted decision-making
- Marketing education

The project is designed for continuous evolution.

Version 1.0 represents the beginning, not the end.
