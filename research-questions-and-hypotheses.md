# Case Background

Telex Soluções Auditivas redesigned its lead acquisition and customer journey process between 2024 and 2025.

The previous model was centered on a call center that received leads, contacted customers, mediated scheduling with stores and confirmed appointments.

The redesigned model reduced lead capture friction, implemented a WhatsApp bot, connected the flow to CRM and automation tools, and transferred lead ownership to store-level CSO teams.

The observed results included:

* increase in lead volume
* faster initial interaction
* shorter scheduling time
* higher appointment attendance
* higher sales volume
* lower estimated operational cost per conversion
* increased number of low-quality or ghost leads

---

# Research Problem

The central problem of this case is the presence of friction and operational inefficiency in the customer journey between lead generation and appointment conversion.

In the original process, the customer showed interest through digital campaigns but had to pass through a centralized call center that mediated communication between the customer and the store.

This structure created delays, unclear ownership, incentive misalignment, customer distrust and potential lead loss.

The redesigned process sought to reduce friction by simplifying the entry flow, automating the first interaction and transferring operational responsibility to the store.

# Research Hypotheses

The hypotheses below are designed to guide analysis and discussion.

They should be interpreted according to the limitations of the case study methodology.

---

# H1 — Friction Reduction and Lead Volume

## Hypothesis

Reducing friction in the lead capture process increases the volume of leads generated.

## Rationale

The previous model required more information before the customer could progress.

The redesigned model reduced the initial data requirement to name, city and desired store, making the first step easier.

## Expected Relationship

```text
Lower entry friction → Higher lead volume
```

## Related Indicators

* leads generated in 2024
* leads generated in 2025
* lead capture format before and after redesign

## Available Evidence

Lead volume increased substantially after the simplification of the entry flow.

## Methodological Caution

Lead growth may also have been influenced by media investment, campaign optimization, seasonality or brand activity.

---

# H2 — Automation and Response Time

## Hypothesis

The use of chatbot-based initial interaction reduces response time after customer entry.

## Rationale

In the 2024 model, response depended on call center availability and business hours.

In the 2025 model, the bot responded immediately after WhatsApp entry.

## Expected Relationship

```text
Chatbot entry → Faster initial response
```

## Related Indicators

* response time in 2024
* response time in 2025
* availability of bot interaction
* service coverage outside call center hours

## Available Evidence

The initial response shifted from approximately 20 minutes during business hours to immediate bot interaction.

## Methodological Caution

Immediate bot response does not necessarily mean immediate human service.

The analysis must distinguish automated response from human follow-up.

---

# H3 — Decentralized Ownership and Scheduling Speed

## Hypothesis

Transferring lead ownership to store-level CSO teams reduces the time required to schedule appointments.

## Rationale

The 2024 model required call center mediation between customer and store.

The 2025 model allowed the store to contact the lead directly and manage scheduling according to local agenda availability.

## Expected Relationship

```text
Store-level ownership → Faster scheduling
```

## Related Indicators

* time until scheduling in 2024
* time until scheduling in 2025
* call center role before and after redesign
* store CSO responsibilities

## Available Evidence

Scheduling time decreased from up to 48 hours to up to 2 hours.

## Methodological Caution

Store-level performance may vary by unit, CSO availability and local management quality.

---

# H4 — Direct Store Contact and Attendance Rate

## Hypothesis

Direct store contact improves appointment attendance rate.

## Rationale

When the store assumes responsibility for the lead, communication becomes more directly connected to the actual service location.

This may increase trust, clarity and commitment.

## Expected Relationship

```text
Direct store contact → Higher appointment attendance
```

## Related Indicators

* appointments scheduled
* attendance
* appointment-to-attendance conversion rate
* store ownership model

## Available Evidence

The appointment-to-attendance rate increased after the redesign.

## Methodological Caution

Attendance may also be affected by reminders, customer profile, campaign targeting, regional factors or store execution quality.

---

# H5 — Journey Redesign and Sales Conversion

## Hypothesis

A faster and more store-centered customer journey improves sales conversion performance.

## Rationale

Reducing delay and increasing store ownership may allow the customer to reach the hearing check-up with greater clarity, commitment and trust.

## Expected Relationship

```text
Faster journey + store ownership → Higher sales conversion
```

## Related Indicators

* attendance
* sales
* attendance-to-sale conversion rate
* lead-to-sale conversion rate

## Available Evidence

Sales volume and conversion rates increased after the redesign.

## Methodological Caution

Sales performance may be influenced by factors not isolated in the case, such as pricing, promotions, sales training, campaign quality, media investment and product availability.

---

# H6 — Reduced Friction and Lead Quality Trade-Off

## Hypothesis

Reducing lead capture friction increases the number of low-quality or ghost leads.

## Rationale

When fewer fields are required at entry, more customers start the flow, including those with lower intent or incomplete interest.

## Expected Relationship

```text
Lower entry friction → Higher lead volume + More low-quality leads
```

## Related Indicators

* lead volume
* ghost lead rate
* non-response rate
* cold lead rate
* store follow-up outcomes

## Available Evidence

The redesigned model generated more leads but also increased the number of ghost leads and non-responsive leads.

## Methodological Caution

The current dataset needs more granular classification of lead quality to test this hypothesis precisely.

---

# H7 — CRM Automation and Operational Scalability

## Hypothesis

CRM automation improves operational scalability by allowing the organization to process more leads with fewer call center resources.

## Rationale

The 2025 model reduced the number of operators while increasing lead volume and sales output.

Automation distributed lead data through RD Conversas, RD CRM, Google Sheets and Pluga integrations.

## Expected Relationship

```text
CRM automation → Higher processing capacity with lower operational dependency
```

## Related Indicators

* number of call center operators
* lead volume
* appointments
* sales
* estimated operational cost
* system stack before and after redesign

## Available Evidence

The operation processed a much larger volume of leads with fewer call center operators and lower estimated operational cost.

## Methodological Caution

Operational cost estimates exclude benefits, taxes, paid media investment and indirect overhead.

# Recommended Main Problem Statement for TCC

Apesar do crescimento dos canais digitais de aquisição, a conversão de leads em vendas depende da capacidade organizacional de transformar interesse inicial em atendimento efetivo. No caso da Telex Soluções Auditivas, a jornada anterior apresentava fricções operacionais entre a entrada do lead, o call center e as lojas, resultando em atrasos, perda de leads e baixa eficiência do funil. Diante disso, este estudo analisa como a simplificação da entrada, a automação via chatbot e a transferência de ownership para as lojas influenciaram os indicadores de agendamento, comparecimento e vendas.

---

# Recommended General Objective for TCC

Analisar como a simplificação da jornada do cliente, combinada à automação de CRM e à descentralização do atendimento para as lojas, impactou os indicadores de geração de leads, agendamento, comparecimento e vendas na Telex Soluções Auditivas.

3. Comparar os indicadores de funil comercial antes e depois da intervenção.

4. Avaliar os efeitos da redução do tempo de resposta e do tempo de agendamento sobre o desempenho do funil.

5. Discutir os trade-offs entre aumento de volume de leads e aumento de leads de baixa qualidade.

6. Apontar implicações gerenciais e acadêmicas do redesenho da jornada em empresas de serviços B2C.

# Methodological Cautions

The hypotheses should not be treated as definitive causal proof unless supported by stronger statistical controls.

The study should explicitly acknowledge:

* absence of randomized control group
* possible media investment variation
* possible seasonality
* store-level performance variation
* campaign quality changes
* incomplete lead quality classification
* incomplete CAC calculation
* external commercial influences
