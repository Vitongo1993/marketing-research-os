# Purpose

This document organizes the main authors that may support the theoretical framework, methodology and discussion of the Telex Soluções Auditivas MBA Final Project.

Its purpose is to connect each author to a specific theoretical area and explain how their work may help interpret the Telex case.

This file supports:

* `literature-review-map.md`
* `tcc-outline.md`
* `references/books.md`

* `references/abnt-references.md`

# How to Use This Document

This document should be used as a reference map.

It does not replace the final bibliography.

Before using any author in the TCC, the researcher should verify:

* exact publication title
* year of publication
* edition
* publisher or journal
* ABNT formatting
* relevance to the specific argument being made

# 1. Customer Journey Authors

This group supports the analysis of journey stages, touchpoints, friction, customer decision paths and the transition from digital interest to physical service attendance.

---

## Katherine N. Lemon

## Main Area

Customer Journey and Customer Experience.

## Relevance to the TCC

Lemon is relevant for explaining how customer journeys involve multiple touchpoints across time and channels.

Her work helps support the idea that the Telex case should not be analyzed only as a lead generation problem, but as an end-to-end journey problem.

## Possible Use in the TCC

Use Lemon to support discussions about:

* customer journey structure
* touchpoints
* customer experience across channels
* digital-to-physical journey
* journey redesign

## Connection to Telex

The Telex journey involved multiple touchpoints:

```text id="8qaz8v"
Ad
   ↓
WhatsApp
   ↓
Bot
   ↓
CRM
   ↓
Store Contact
   ↓
Appointment
   ↓
Attendance
   ↓
Sale
```

Lemon helps justify analyzing these interactions as one integrated journey.

# Critical Citation Rule

Do not use authors only to make the TCC look more academic.

Every citation must support a clear argument.

Before using an author, ask:

```text id="49iy1n"
Does this author help explain the Telex case?
```

If the answer is no, the author should not be used.
