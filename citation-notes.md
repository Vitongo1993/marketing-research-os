# How to Use This Document

This file should be used during the writing of the TCC to decide:

* where each author should be cited
* which argument each reference supports
* which chapter each source belongs to
* how to avoid decorative citations
* how to connect theory directly to the Telex case

This document is not a substitute for reading the original sources.

It is a strategic citation guide.

---

# Core Citation Principle

Every citation must support a specific argument.

Before citing any author, ask:

```text id="s2k1va"
What exact idea does this reference support in the Telex case?
```

If the answer is unclear, the citation should not be used.

---

# Citation Logic for the TCC

The TCC should use citations to support five main analytical blocks:

```text id="njs3jf"
1. Customer journey and friction
2. Customer experience and service quality
3. CRM and lead management
4. Automation, chatbot and AI-supported interaction
5. Methodology and case study design
```

Each block should connect directly to the research problem:

```text id="sa8p38"
How did the redesign of the customer journey, CRM flow and lead management process affect lead generation, appointment scheduling, attendance and sales conversion at Telex Soluções Auditivas?
```

# 10. Causal Language Rules

Because this study is based on a before-and-after case analysis, citations should not be used to overstate causality.

---

## Avoid

```text id="rg0ir5"
The redesign caused the increase in sales.
```

## Prefer

```text id="t98x29"
The data suggest that the redesign was associated with an increase in sales.
```

---

## Avoid

```text id="rxraf3"
The chatbot proved to be responsible for improved conversion.
```

## Prefer

```text id="il5dx3"
The chatbot-based entry coincided with faster initial response and may have contributed to improved funnel performance when combined with CRM routing and store-level ownership.
```

---

## Avoid

```text id="uqr2wy"
Store ownership caused higher attendance.
```

## Prefer

```text id="risyg1"
The increase in attendance rate may be associated with faster scheduling, direct store contact and clearer lead ownership.
```

---

# 11. Recommended Citation Density

Avoid excessive citation density.

A good structure is:

* one or two citations for each key concept
* one citation when defining a theory
* one citation when connecting theory to the case
* no citation for every sentence

Recommended density:

```text id="m24exv"
Introduction: moderate citation density
Theoretical Framework: high citation density
Methodology: moderate citation density
Case Context: low to moderate citation density
Results and Discussion: moderate citation density
Managerial Implications: low to moderate citation density
Conclusion: low citation density
```

---

# 12. Final Citation Strategy

The strongest final citation strategy is:

```text id="oiio58"
Use academic literature to define concepts.
Use case data to show what happened.
Use reports to contextualize market relevance.
Use methodology authors to limit causal claims.
Use managerial literature to explain implications.
```
