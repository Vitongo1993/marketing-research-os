# 1. Customer Journey Articles

---

## Lemon and Verhoef — Understanding Customer Experience Throughout the Customer Journey

## Reference Type

Academic article.

## Suggested Full Reference

Lemon, K. N.; Verhoef, P. C. Understanding customer experience throughout the customer journey. *Journal of Marketing*, 2016.

## Main Area

Customer Journey and Customer Experience.

## Core Contribution

This article is one of the most important references for the TCC.

It explains customer experience as something formed across multiple touchpoints and stages of the customer journey.

## Possible Use in the TCC

Use this article to support:

* customer journey definition
* customer experience across touchpoints
* journey stages
* digital and physical interaction
* accumulated customer perception
* journey-based analysis

## Connection to Telex

The Telex case involves several connected stages:

```text id="yhh0iq"
Ad
   ↓
WhatsApp
   ↓
Bot
   ↓
CRM
   ↓
Store Contact
   ↓
Appointment
   ↓
Attendance
   ↓
Sale
```

This article supports the argument that these stages should be analyzed as an integrated customer journey, not as isolated operational steps.

## Recommended Priority

Essential.

---

## Rawson, Duncan and Jones — The Truth About Customer Experience

## Reference Type

Professional article.

## Suggested Full Reference

Rawson, A.; Duncan, E.; Jones, C. The truth about customer experience. *Harvard Business Review*, 2013.

## Main Area

End-to-End Customer Experience.

## Core Contribution

This article argues that organizations should manage customer experience across complete journeys, rather than optimizing isolated touchpoints.

## Possible Use in the TCC

Use this article to support:

* end-to-end customer journey
* cross-functional journey management
* journey friction
* operational alignment
* customer experience consistency

## Connection to Telex

The old Telex model had friction between call center and stores.

This article supports the argument that a good experience depends on the coordination of the full journey.

## Recommended Priority

Essential.

## Davenport, Guha, Grewal and Bressgott — How Artificial Intelligence Will Change the Future of Marketing

## Reference Type

Academic article.

## Suggested Full Reference

Davenport, T.; Guha, A.; Grewal, D.; Bressgott, T. How artificial intelligence will change the future of marketing. *Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science*, 2020.

## Main Area

Artificial Intelligence in Marketing.

## Core Contribution

This article presents a framework for understanding the impact of artificial intelligence on marketing strategy and customer behavior.

It also emphasizes that AI may be more effective when it augments, rather than fully replaces, human managers.

## Possible Use in the TCC

Use this article to support:

* AI in marketing
* automation
* human-AI collaboration
* decision support
* marketing transformation
* ethical and managerial implications

## Connection to Telex

The Telex case uses automation to accelerate entry and routing, while human CSOs remain responsible for relationship-building, scheduling and sales.

## Recommended Priority

Essential.
