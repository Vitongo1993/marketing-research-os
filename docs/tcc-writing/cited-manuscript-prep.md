# Core Rule

The final manuscript should not be overloaded with citations.

Citations should be inserted only where they support a clear academic function:

```text id="c8sanr"
1. Defining a concept.
2. Justifying a method.
3. Supporting an interpretation.
4. Connecting theory to the Telex case.
5. Establishing methodological caution.
6. Contextualizing a sector or trend.
```

Do not cite internal Telex data as if it were academic theory.

Do not cite academic authors for operational facts that come from the case.

The final manuscript will use three types of evidence.

---

## 1. Academic Citations

Use for:

```text id="zpbuvt"

methodology

```

Example:

```text id="hmh218"

```

---

## 2. Internal Case Data

Use for:

```text id="joyv9a"
Telex indicators
2024 and 2025 funnel data
operational flow
cost estimates
business rules
tool stack
call center responsibilities
store ownership changes
```

Example:

```text id="s8ge6h"
Os leads gerados passaram de 4.560 em 2024 para 24.789 em 2025, segundo dados operacionais internos da empresa.
```

Suggested source label in tables:

```text id="ki35zr"
Fonte: Elaborado pelo autor com base em dados operacionais internos da Telex Soluções Auditivas.
```

Use only if company authorization is confirmed.

---

## 3. Analytical Interpretation

Use for:

```text id="n733uu"
association between redesign and funnel improvement
interpretation of friction reduction
connection between CRM automation and operational scalability
discussion of trade-offs
managerial implications
```

Example:

```text id="j6uaju"
Esses resultados sugerem associação entre o redesenho da jornada e a melhora dos indicadores do funil, embora não permitam afirmar causalidade definitiva.
```

This type of statement should often combine theory and case data.
