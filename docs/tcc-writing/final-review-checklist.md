# Purpose

This document provides the final review checklist for the MBA Final Project before assembling, formatting and submitting the final version.

Its purpose is to ensure that the TCC is academically coherent, methodologically defensible, data-consistent, properly cited, correctly formatted and aligned with the research objective.

This checklist should be used after the main chapter drafts, citation plan, tables and figures list, abstract, keywords and ABNT checklist have been created.

---

# Review Philosophy

The final review should not focus only on grammar or formatting.

The final review must verify whether the TCC works as one coherent academic argument.

The central question is:

```text id="34ybsp"
Does the final TCC clearly explain how the redesign of the customer journey, CRM flow and lead management process was associated with changes in lead generation, scheduling, attendance and sales conversion at Telex Soluções Auditivas?
```

If any chapter does not help answer this question, it should be revised.

# 16. Final Argument Review

```text id="464wrp"

CRM and automation supported routing and scale.
```

# Final Summary

This final review checklist should be used as the last quality-control document before assembling the final manuscript and preparing the submission version.

```text id="rcmq9d"
1. Coherence between problem, objectives, method, results and conclusion.
2. Consistency of all Telex data.
3. Proper citation of theory and methodology.
4. Cautious interpretation of before-and-after results.
5. Clear separation between operational cost and CAC.
```

The TCC should be submitted only when the document is coherent as a whole, not only when each chapter seems complete individually.
