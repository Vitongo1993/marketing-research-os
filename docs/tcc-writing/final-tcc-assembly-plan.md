# Chapter Transition Plan

Each chapter should end with a transition to the next one.

---

## Introduction to Theoretical Framework

Suggested transition:

```text id="wmku5u"
After presenting the research problem, objectives and justification, the next chapter introduces the theoretical foundations that support the analysis of customer journey, CRM, lead management, automation and service marketing.
```

---

## Theoretical Framework to Methodology

Suggested transition:

```text id="pjtlql"
Based on these theoretical foundations, the next chapter presents the methodological procedures adopted to analyze the Telex case.
```

---

## Methodology to Case Context

Suggested transition:

```text id="fwk74v"
After defining the research design, the next chapter presents the organizational and operational context of the case analyzed.
```

---

## Case Context to Before-and-After Analysis

Suggested transition:

```text id="xg2ui3"
Once the case context and the operational redesign have been described, the next chapter compares the 2024 and 2025 models in greater detail.
```

---

## Before-and-After Analysis to Results and Discussion

Suggested transition:

```text id="jkakkm"
The comparison between the two models provides the basis for the following chapter, which discusses the main quantitative and qualitative results of the redesign.
```

---

## Results and Discussion to Managerial Implications

Suggested transition:

```text id="alg2cv"
Based on the results observed, the next chapter discusses the main managerial implications for Telex and for similar service-based organizations.
```

---

## Managerial Implications to Limitations

Suggested transition:

```text id="hho1ig"
Although the case provides relevant managerial insights, the interpretation of the results must consider methodological and data limitations, presented in the next chapter.
```

---

## Limitations to Conclusion

Suggested transition:

```text id="s85sq5"
Finally, the conclusion summarizes the study, answers the research question and presents the main academic and managerial contributions.
```

# Final Data Validation Checklist

Before final assembly, validate the following:

```text id="w5ol0j"
1. Are the 2024 and 2025 periods equivalent?
2. Does 2025 represent a full year, partial year or projection?
3. Were the KPIs extracted from the same source?
4. Are leads counted the same way in both periods?
5. Are appointments counted the same way in both periods?
6. Are attended appointments counted consistently?
7. Are sales counted consistently?
8. Are canceled appointments excluded or included?
9. Are duplicated leads removed?
10. Are bot-abandoned contacts counted as leads?
11. Is the cost calculation operational only?
12. Are media costs excluded?
13. Are store costs excluded?
14. Are benefits and taxes excluded?
15. Is the limitation clearly stated?
```

# Final Writing Rules

The final document should follow these writing rules:

```text id="uzbtab"
1. Use academic language.
2. Avoid promotional language.
3. Avoid excessive repetition.
4. Avoid unsupported causal claims.
5. Connect every result to the research question.
6. Connect every theoretical concept to the case.
7. Use tables for numerical clarity.
8. Use figures only when they improve comprehension.
9. Keep the conclusion concise.
10. Do not introduce new data in the conclusion.
```
