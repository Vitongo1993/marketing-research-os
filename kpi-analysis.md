# Purpose

This document presents the quantitative analysis of the Telex Soluções Auditivas case study.

It evaluates the performance impact of the customer journey, CRM and lead management redesign implemented between 2024 and 2025.

The analysis focuses on:

* lead generation
* appointment scheduling
* attendance
* sales conversion
* response time
* scheduling time
* operational cost efficiency
* funnel performance
* limitations of interpretation

---

# Source Note

The indicators analyzed in this document are based on internal operational data provided for the Telex case study.

The values should be treated as business case data and must be validated against official CRM, media and sales reports before final academic publication.

# Funnel Interpretation

## 1. Lead Generation Scale

The number of leads increased from 4,560 in 2024 to 24,789 in 2025.

This represents a growth of approximately 443.6%.

This result suggests that simplifying the entry flow and reducing form friction substantially expanded the volume of customer interactions.

However, this growth must be interpreted alongside the increase in lower-quality leads and ghost leads.

---

## 2. Appointment Scheduling

Appointments increased from 4,552 in 2024 to 24,757 in 2025.

The lead-to-appointment rate remained extremely high in both periods:

* 2024: 99.82%
* 2025: 99.87%

This indicates that the largest transformation did not occur in the lead-to-appointment percentage itself, but in the system's ability to process a much larger volume of leads.

---

## 3. Attendance Improvement

Attendance increased from 2,415 to 15,785.

The appointment-to-attendance rate increased from 53.05% to 63.76%.

This is one of the most strategically relevant findings of the case.

It suggests that faster scheduling, store-level ownership and clearer communication may have improved the customer's commitment to attending the hearing check-up.

---

## 4. Sales Conversion Improvement

Sales increased from 104 to 1,288.

The attendance-to-sale conversion rate increased from 4.31% to 8.16%.

This suggests that the redesigned model not only increased the number of people reaching the store but also improved the commercial efficiency of the attendance stage.

This result should be interpreted carefully because sales may also have been influenced by campaign quality, promotional conditions, media investment, store execution, pricing or training.

---

## 5. Lead-to-Sale Efficiency

The lead-to-sale conversion rate increased from 2.28% to 5.20%.

This indicates that the redesigned system generated more leads and became more efficient at transforming leads into sales.

The improvement suggests that reducing initial friction did not merely increase volume; it improved the overall funnel output.

# Scheduling Time Analysis

| Indicator             |      2024 |     2025 |
| --------------------- | --------: | -------: |
| Time Until Scheduling | Up to 48h | Up to 2h |

The scheduling time was reduced from up to 48 hours to up to 2 hours.

This represents an approximate reduction of 95.8% in maximum scheduling time.

This is strategically important because time delay is a major friction point in service-based customer journeys.

The shorter the distance between intent and scheduling, the lower the probability of lead cooling, abandonment or loss of trust.

# Operational Cost Analysis

## 2024 Cost Structure

### People

* 1 supervisor: R$ 7,000/month + benefits
* 6 operators: R$ 1,800/month each + benefits
* bonus per appointment: R$ 10
* bonus per sale: R$ 70

### Systems

* RD CRM: R$ 1,900/month
* Bot Conversas: R$ 952/month

---

## 2025 Cost Structure

### People

* 3 operators: R$ 2,800/month each + benefits

### Systems

* RD CRM: R$ 400/month
* RD Conversas: R$ 3,352/month
* Pluga Integrations: R$ 900/year

---

# Estimated Operational Cost

> This estimate excludes employee benefits, taxes, paid media investment, management overhead and indirect costs.

## 2024 Estimated Monthly Cost

```text
Supervisor salary: R$ 7,000
Operators salary: 6 × R$ 1,800 = R$ 10,800
RD CRM: R$ 1,900
Bot Conversas: R$ 952
Estimated fixed monthly cost: R$ 20,652
```

## 2024 Estimated Variable Compensation

```text
Appointments: 4,552 × R$ 10 = R$ 45,520/year
Sales: 104 × R$ 70 = R$ 7,280/year
Total variable compensation: R$ 52,800/year
Estimated monthly variable compensation: R$ 4,400
```

## 2024 Estimated Total Monthly Cost

```text
R$ 20,652 + R$ 4,400 = R$ 25,052/month
```

## 2024 Estimated Annual Cost

```text
R$ 25,052 × 12 = R$ 300,624/year
```

---

## 2025 Estimated Monthly Cost

```text
Operators salary: 3 × R$ 2,800 = R$ 8,400
RD CRM: R$ 400
RD Conversas: R$ 3,352
Pluga: R$ 900/year = R$ 75/month
Estimated monthly cost: R$ 12,227/month
```

## 2025 Estimated Annual Cost

```text
R$ 12,227 × 12 = R$ 146,724/year
```

# Strategic Cost Interpretation

The operational cost layer became significantly more efficient after the redesign.

The estimated annual operational cost decreased from R$ 300,624 to R$ 146,724, while total sales increased from 104 to 1,288.

This suggests that the redesigned model achieved two simultaneous effects:

1. lower estimated operational cost
2. higher output volume

However, this should not be interpreted as full CAC reduction, because media investment and other commercial costs were not included.

# KPI Interpretation by MROS Framework

## Customer Journey Framework

The most relevant improvement occurred in the transition between:

```text
Intent → Conversion
```

The redesign reduced friction by:

* simplifying entry
* decreasing response delay
* routing leads directly to stores
* reducing unnecessary intermediation

---

## CRM Framework

The CRM became more operational.

Instead of only storing data, it became part of the lead movement system:

```text
Lead Entry → CRM → Store Sheet → Automated Email → CSO Action
```

This transformed CRM from a passive reporting tool into a coordination mechanism.

---

## Lead Management Framework

The lead management process improved because:

* lead ownership became clearer
* routing became faster
* the call center stopped being the default bottleneck
* the store became responsible for scheduling and follow-up

---

# Key Findings

## Finding 1 — Friction Reduction Increased Scale

The reduction of form complexity and the adoption of WhatsApp-based entry increased lead volume substantially.

---

## Finding 2 — Speed Improved Funnel Continuity

The reduction in scheduling time from up to 48h to up to 2h likely contributed to improved attendance rates.

---

## Finding 3 — Store Ownership Improved Operational Alignment

The store had direct access to its agenda and became responsible for customer relationship continuity.

This reduced the operational mismatch between centralized scheduling and local service availability.

---

## Finding 4 — Automation Reduced Operational Dependency

The use of RD Conversas, RD CRM, Google Sheets and Pluga reduced manual intermediation and created a more scalable lead distribution system.

---

## Finding 5 — Volume Growth Came with Lead Quality Trade-Offs

The system generated more leads, but also increased:

* ghost leads
* low-quality leads
* leads who did not respond after being sent to stores

This trade-off must be managed through improved classification and follow-up rules.

---

# Methodological Cautions

## 1. The Case Is Not a Controlled Experiment

The data compares two operational periods but does not isolate all external factors.

Causality should be discussed carefully.

---

## 2. Media Investment Is Missing

Without paid media investment, full CAC cannot be calculated.

The cost analysis only evaluates the operational layer.

---

## 3. Store-Level Variation Is Unknown

Different stores may have different performance levels.

Future analysis should compare:

* response time by store
* attendance by store
* sales by store
* conversion by CSO
* lead quality by region

---

## 4. Lead Quality Needs Further Classification

The increase in ghost leads must be quantified.

Future versions should include:

* number of ghost leads
* percentage of non-responsive leads
* qualification rate
* invalid contact rate
* lost lead rate

# Executive KPI Summary

The 2025 redesign produced a stronger operational funnel.

The most important improvements were:

* lead volume increased 443.6%
* attendance volume increased 553.6%
* sales volume increased 1,138.5%
* scheduling time decreased from up to 48h to up to 2h
* estimated cost per sale decreased 96.1%
* lead-to-sale conversion increased from 2.28% to 5.20%

The results suggest that simplifying the customer journey and decentralizing lead ownership created a more scalable and efficient conversion system.

---

# Final Interpretation

The Telex case provides evidence that customer journey redesign should not be limited to marketing communication.

The key transformation occurred in the operational architecture connecting:

* acquisition
* CRM
* routing
* store ownership
* scheduling
* sales execution

The new model improved both scale and efficiency by reducing friction and aligning operational responsibility with the point of service delivery.
