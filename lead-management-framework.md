# Purpose

This document defines the **Lead Management Framework (LMF)** within the Marketing Research OS.

It establishes how leads are:

* captured
* qualified
* categorized
* routed
* prioritized
* tracked across the customer journey

The framework connects marketing acquisition systems with CRM execution and sales conversion processes.

---

# Core Concept

A lead is not just a contact.

In the MROS model, a lead is defined as:

> **a dynamic entity representing a potential customer with evolving intent signals across time and interactions.**

---

# Lead Lifecycle Model

```text
Capture
   ↓
Identification
   ↓
Qualification
   ↓
Routing
   ↓
Engagement
   ↓
Conversion or Disqualification
```

Each stage must be measurable and integrated into CRM systems.

---

# Stage 1 — Capture

## Objective

Collect initial lead data from acquisition channels.

## Sources

* Paid ads
* Landing pages
* WhatsApp entry points
* Organic traffic
* Referrals

## Principle

Data should be minimal and conversion-oriented.

---

# Stage 2 — Identification

## Objective

Structure raw inputs into identifiable lead records.

## Key Data Points

* Name
* Contact
* Location
* Source channel

## Outcome

A unique lead profile in the CRM system.

---

# Stage 3 — Qualification

## Objective

Evaluate lead potential and intent level.

## Classification

* Cold Lead (low intent)
* Warm Lead (moderate intent)
* Hot Lead (high intent)

## Criteria

* engagement level
* response behavior
* explicit intent signals
* demographic fit

---

# Stage 4 — Routing

## Objective

Assign the lead to the correct destination.

## Routing Logic

* nearest store
* available CSO (sales agent)
* specialized team (if needed)

## Critical Principle

> Every lead must have a clear owner.

---

# Stage 5 — Engagement

## Objective

Maintain active communication until decision stage.

## Channels

* WhatsApp
* phone
* chatbot
* in-person contact

## Key Rule

Response time directly impacts conversion probability.

---

# Stage 6 — Conversion or Disqualification

## Outcomes

### Conversion

* appointment scheduled
* product tested
* purchase completed

### Disqualification

* no response
* no fit
* lack of interest
* logistical constraints

Lead Management is fully embedded in CRM systems.

Each lead must include:

* lifecycle stage

* interaction history
* status updates
* conversion probability score

---

# Telex Soluções Auditivas — Applied Insight

## Core Problem Identified

* excessive centralization in call center
* lack of real-time store integration
* inefficient lead handoff
* delayed response cycles

## Structural Failure Point

> Qualification → Routing

Leads were delayed and filtered through unnecessary intermediaries.

---

## Implemented Solution

* chatbot-based pre-qualification
* simplified input structure
* direct store routing
* CSO ownership per store
* reduced intermediary handling

---

## Observed Outcome

* increased lead volume
* improved conversion efficiency
* reduced drop-off rate
* faster response time
