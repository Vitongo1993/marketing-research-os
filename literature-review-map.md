# Purpose

This document defines the literature review map for the Telex Soluções Auditivas MBA Final Project.

Its purpose is to organize the main theoretical areas, authors, concepts and bibliographic directions that will support the analysis of the customer journey redesign, CRM automation, lead management and funnel performance.

This document is not the final theoretical framework.

It is a map that guides which authors, books, articles and concepts should be researched and later incorporated into the TCC.

---

# Role in the TCC

The literature review will support the following parts of the TCC:

```text id="rvktv3"
Chapter 2 — Theoretical Framework
Chapter 3 — Methodology
Chapter 6 — Results and Discussion
Chapter 7 — Managerial Implications
Chapter 10 — References
```

The literature review should connect theory to the Telex case.

It should not be a generic summary of Marketing concepts.

Every theoretical section must help explain some aspect of the case:

* customer journey friction
* lead conversion
* CRM structure
* lead management
* automation
* chatbot-based interaction
* customer experience
* service marketing
* sales funnel performance
* digital-to-physical conversion

## Connection to Telex

This section should help explain why the previous journey created friction.

The old model had several touchpoints and handoffs:

```text id="hqjs04"
Lead form
   ↓
Call center
   ↓
Store
   ↓
Call center
   ↓
Customer
```

The new model reduced the path and created faster routing:

```text id="ptkfph"
WhatsApp
   ↓
Bot
   ↓
CRM
   ↓
Store
   ↓
Customer
```

The literature should support the idea that friction and unnecessary handoffs may reduce customer progression in the journey.

# Citation Strategy

The final TCC should not cite every author listed in this map.

The recommended approach is to select the most relevant authors for each section.

A strong but manageable structure would include:

```text id="czb1wx"
Customer Journey: Lemon and Verhoef; Rawson, Duncan and Jones
Customer Experience: Meyer and Schwager; Parasuraman, Zeithaml and Berry
CRM: Payne and Frow; Buttle and Maklan
Lead Management: Järvinen and Taiminen
AI and Automation: Davenport et al.; Huang and Rust
Service Marketing: Zeithaml, Bitner and Gremler; Grönroos
Marketing Metrics: Farris et al.
Methodology: Yin; Creswell; Gil
```

This prevents the theoretical framework from becoming too broad or unfocused.

---

# Critical Integration Rule

Every cited author must help explain the Telex case.

Avoid citing authors only to "fill space."

Before adding a citation, ask:

```text id="sce0f3"
Does this author help explain the relationship between journey redesign, CRM automation, lead management and funnel performance?
```

If the answer is no, the citation should not be included.
