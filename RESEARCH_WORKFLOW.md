# Purpose

The purpose of this document is to establish the official scientific research workflow adopted by the **Marketing Research OS (MROS)**.

This workflow provides a structured, evidence-based and AI-assisted methodology that guides researchers from the identification of a research problem to the publication of scientific results.

Rather than prescribing a rigid sequence of activities, this workflow defines a repeatable process that promotes scientific rigor, transparency, reproducibility and continuous improvement.

---

# Guiding Principles

Every research conducted using the Marketing Research OS must follow the principles defined in:

* ADR-0001

# Research Lifecycle

The official MROS workflow is composed of ten stages.

```text
Research Problem
        │
        ▼
Literature Review
        │
        ▼
Critical Analysis
        │
        ▼
Research Design
        │
        ▼
Data Collection
        │
        ▼
Data Analysis
        │
        ▼
Discussion
        │
        ▼
Scientific Writing
        │
        ▼
Peer Review
        │
        ▼
Publication & Continuous Improvement
```

Each stage produces specific deliverables and may be revisited whenever new evidence emerges.

---

# Stage 1 — Research Problem

## Objective

Clearly define the research problem.

## Key Questions

* What is the real problem?
* Why is it relevant?
* Who is affected?
* What evidence suggests that the problem exists?

## Deliverables

* Problem statement
* Research context
* Initial assumptions
* Research motivation

## AI Support

Artificial Intelligence may assist by:

* generating exploratory questions;
* identifying possible research gaps;
* organizing contextual information.

Human validation is mandatory.

---

# Stage 2 — Literature Review

## Objective

Understand the current state of knowledge.

## Activities

* Search academic databases.
* Identify foundational authors.
* Analyze recent publications.
* Compare theoretical perspectives.
* Map knowledge gaps.

## Deliverables

* Literature matrix
* Reference database
* Concept map
* Initial theoretical framework

## Preferred Sources

1. Peer-reviewed articles
2. Academic books
3. Systematic reviews
4. Meta-analyses
5. Official reports

---

# Stage 3 — Critical Analysis

## Objective

Transform information into knowledge.

Researchers should:

* compare theories;
* identify contradictions;
* evaluate methodological quality;
* recognize limitations;
* formulate preliminary interpretations.

## Deliverables

* Critical synthesis
* Identified gaps
* Initial hypotheses

---

# Stage 4 — Research Design

## Objective

Define how the research will be conducted.

Possible components include:

* research objectives;
* research questions;
* hypotheses;
* methodological approach;
* sampling strategy;
* data collection methods;
* ethical considerations.

## Deliverables

* Research protocol
* Methodology chapter
* Data collection plan

---

# Stage 5 — Data Collection

## Objective

Collect reliable information according to the research design.

Possible techniques include:

* interviews;
* questionnaires;
* observations;
* document analysis;
* secondary databases;
* business reports.

## Deliverables

* Raw data
* Interview transcripts
* Survey responses
* Research log

---

# Stage 6 — Data Analysis

## Objective

Generate meaningful insights from collected data.

Possible methods include:

* descriptive statistics;
* thematic analysis;
* content analysis;
* regression models;
* comparative analysis;
* triangulation.

## Deliverables

* Analytical results
* Tables
* Figures
* Visualizations
* Statistical outputs

---

# Stage 7 — Discussion

## Objective

Interpret findings in light of the literature.

Researchers should answer:

* What do the results mean?
* Do they support existing theories?
* Do they challenge previous findings?
* What practical implications emerge?

## Deliverables

* Discussion chapter
* Managerial implications
* Academic contributions
* Limitations

---

# Stage 8 — Scientific Writing

## Objective

Transform research into a coherent scientific document.

The writing process should prioritize:

* logical organization;
* methodological transparency;
* argumentative consistency;
* proper citation;
* academic language.

## Deliverables

* Final manuscript
* Figures
* References
* Appendices

---

# Stage 9 — Peer Review

## Objective

Improve research quality through critical evaluation.

Recommended reviewers include:

* academic advisors;
* colleagues;
* subject specialists;
* AI-assisted review (with human validation).

Review criteria:

* clarity;
* methodological consistency;
* originality;
* coherence;
* scientific contribution.

---

# Stage 10 — Publication & Continuous Improvement

## Objective

Disseminate knowledge and promote continuous learning.

Possible outputs:

* TCC
* Journal article
* Conference paper
* Technical report
* White paper
* Open educational resources

Research does not end with publication.

New evidence should lead to future revisions and improvements.

---

# AI Integration Layer

Artificial Intelligence may support every stage of the workflow.

Typical applications include:

| Stage              | AI Support                     |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------ |
| Research Problem   | Brainstorming and refinement   |
| Literature Review  | Summarization and organization |
| Critical Analysis  | Comparison of theories         |
| Research Design    | Methodological suggestions     |
| Data Collection    | Instrument drafting            |
| Data Analysis      | Pattern identification         |
| Discussion         | Argument refinement            |
| Scientific Writing | Language improvement           |
| Peer Review        | Consistency checking           |
| Publication        | Formatting and documentation   |

AI outputs must always be critically evaluated by the researcher.

# Workflow Deliverables

By the end of the complete workflow, the researcher should have produced:

* Research problem definition
* Literature review
* Theoretical framework
* Research protocol
* Dataset
* Analytical results
* Discussion
* Scientific manuscript
* Supporting documentation

# Application within MROS

Every module of the Marketing Research OS contributes to one or more stages of this workflow.

Examples:

* `docs/methodology/` → Research Design
* `docs/marketing/` → Literature Review
* `frameworks/` → Research Design
* `prompts/` → AI Integration
* `templates/` → Scientific Writing
* `telex/` → Applied Research

This modular integration ensures that the repository functions as a complete research operating system rather than a collection of isolated documents.

# Continuous Improvement

The workflow should be reviewed whenever:

* significant methodological advances occur;
* new AI capabilities become available;
* governance documents are updated;
* lessons learned from applied research justify improvements.

Continuous refinement is a fundamental characteristic of the Marketing Research OS.
