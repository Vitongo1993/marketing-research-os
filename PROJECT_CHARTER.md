# 1. Executive Summary

The **Marketing Research OS (MROS)** is an open knowledge framework created to support scientific research in Marketing through a structured combination of academic methodology, practical frameworks and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence.

Unlike traditional manuals, MROS is designed as a modular and continuously evolving knowledge system. It integrates scientific theory, research workflows, prompt engineering, case studies and reusable templates into a single repository.

The project was conceived during the development of the MBA Final Project in Marketing Management at the Federal University of Rio de Janeiro (UFRJ), using the redesign of the customer journey at Telex Soluções Auditivas as its first practical application.

---

# 2. Vision

To become the leading open knowledge framework in Portuguese for AI-assisted Marketing research, recognized for scientific rigor, practical relevance and methodological transparency.

---

# 3. Mission

Empower researchers, students and professionals to produce higher-quality scientific research by combining evidence-based knowledge, structured methodologies and Artificial Intelligence in a responsible manner.

---

# 4. Purpose

The purpose of the Marketing Research OS is to:

- Organize scientific knowledge into reusable modules.
- Reduce friction in academic research workflows.
- Encourage critical thinking.
- Promote ethical use of AI in research.
- Bridge the gap between academic theory and business practice.
- Support continuous learning.

---

# 5. Problem Statement

Scientific research in Marketing is often fragmented.

Researchers commonly face challenges such as:

- dispersed literature;
- inconsistent methodologies;
- repetitive research processes;
- weak integration between AI tools and academic rigor;
- lack of standardized frameworks.

MROS was created to address these challenges.

---

# 6. Project Scope

The project includes:

- Marketing theory
- Customer Relationship Management (CRM)
- Customer Journey
- Customer Experience (CX)
- Growth Marketing
- Consumer Behavior
- Artificial Intelligence
- Research Methodology
- Academic Writing
- ABNT standards
- Prompt Engineering
- Frameworks
- Templates
- Checklists
- Applied Case Studies

---

# 7. Out of Scope

The project does not aim to:

- Replace academic supervision.
- Produce scientific papers automatically.
- Fabricate references.
- Encourage plagiarism.
- Replace human critical thinking.
- Cover disciplines outside its primary scope without clear relevance.

---

# 8. Target Audience

The primary audience includes:

- Undergraduate students
- MBA students
- Master's students
- PhD researchers
- Marketing professionals
- Consultants
- CRM specialists
- Growth professionals
- AI practitioners
- Business researchers

---

# 9. Guiding Principles

The project follows the principles defined in `PROJECT_PRINCIPLES.md`, including:

- Scientific Rigor
- Evidence First
- Critical Thinking
- Transparency
- Human Oversight
- Reproducibility
- Modularity
- Continuous Improvement
- Practical Relevance
- Ethical AI

These principles are mandatory for every module of the repository.

---

# 10. Governance

The repository adopts a documentation-first approach.

Every significant architectural or strategic decision should be documented before implementation.

Governance documents include:

- ADRs
- Project Charter
- Project Principles
- Roadmap
- Contribution Guidelines

---

# 11. Repository Architecture

The repository is organized into independent modules.

Each module must:

- have a clear purpose;
- avoid duplication;
- reference related modules when appropriate;
- remain reusable.

The modular architecture supports continuous growth without compromising consistency.

---

# 12. Documentation Standards

Every document should:

- define its objective;
- identify its intended audience;
- distinguish facts from interpretations;
- include references whenever applicable;
- maintain consistent terminology.

Markdown is the official documentation format.

---

# 13. Artificial Intelligence Policy

Artificial Intelligence is a research assistant.

It must:

- support idea generation;
- organize information;
- assist in synthesis;
- identify knowledge gaps;
- improve productivity.

Artificial Intelligence must never replace scientific judgment.

---

# 14. Quality Standards

All content should be:

- evidence-based;
- traceable;
- understandable;
- reusable;
- maintainable;
- ethically responsible.

Whenever possible, concepts should be supported by peer-reviewed literature.

---

# 15. Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful if it:

- supports the completion of the MBA Final Project;
- becomes reusable for future academic work;
- maintains scientific rigor;
- facilitates AI-assisted research;
- enables continuous expansion without structural redesign.

---

# 16. Risks

Potential risks include:

- repository growth without governance;
- outdated references;
- excessive dependence on AI-generated content;
- inconsistent terminology.

These risks will be mitigated through periodic review and version control.

---

# 17. Long-Term Vision

The Marketing Research OS is intended to evolve beyond the initial MBA project.

Future possibilities include:

- support for Master's and PhD research;
- publication as an open educational resource;
- collaborative contributions;
- integration with AI agents;
- expansion into additional research domains.

---

# 18. Review Policy

This document should be reviewed:

- before each major release;
- whenever the project's mission changes;
- whenever a new governance principle is adopted.

---

# 19. Approval

This charter becomes effective upon publication in the repository.

Future revisions must preserve the project's core principles while allowing continuous improvement.
