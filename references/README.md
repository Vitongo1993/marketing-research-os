# Purpose

This directory organizes the bibliographic foundation of the Marketing Research OS and the Telex Soluções Auditivas MBA Final Project.

Its purpose is to centralize authors, books, academic articles, reports and formatted references used to support the theoretical framework, methodology and discussion of the TCC.

The references should support the following areas:

* customer journey
* customer experience
* CRM
* relationship marketing
* lead management
* sales funnel
* marketing automation
* artificial intelligence in marketing
* service marketing
* marketing metrics
* research methodology

---

# Role in the TCC

The references organized in this directory will support:

```text id="9f4k2s"
Chapter 2 — Theoretical Framework
Chapter 3 — Methodology
Chapter 6 — Results and Discussion
Chapter 10 — References
```

This directory should not be treated as a random bibliography list.

Every reference included here must have a clear connection to the Telex case.

# Reference Selection Criteria

A reference should be included only if it supports at least one of the following:

```text id="aw5v3z"
1. Explains customer journey friction
2. Supports CRM as strategy, process or technology
3. Explains lead management or sales funnel performance
4. Supports marketing automation or AI in customer interaction
5. Explains customer experience in services
6. Supports service marketing theory
7. Supports marketing metrics and funnel analysis
8. Supports the chosen research methodology
```

---

# Evidence Hierarchy

References should be prioritized according to the following hierarchy:

```text id="id0vro"
1. Peer-reviewed academic articles
2. Academic books
3. Methodology books
4. Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
5. Institutional reports
6. Professional publications
7. Blogs and websites only as complementary context
```

---

# Critical Rule

Do not include references only to increase the bibliography size.

Every reference must answer the question:

```text id="xlevin"
How does this source help explain the Telex case?
```

If the reference does not help explain the case, it should not be included.
