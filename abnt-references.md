# ABNT Formatting Logic

The references below follow a simplified ABNT structure:

```text id="ba4x09"
AUTHOR. Title. Journal or Book Title, volume, issue, pages, year.
```

For books:

```text id="cy1xf8"
AUTHOR. Title. Edition. City: Publisher, year.
```

For online or professional sources:

```text id="hl2pgm"
AUTHOR. Title. Publication, year. Available at: [verify link]. Accessed on: [insert access date].
```

The final version should be adapted to the exact ABNT standard required by the MBA program.

# 13. Citation Use Rules

## Rule 1 — Avoid Decorative Citations

Do not cite a source only to make the text appear more academic.

Each citation must support a specific argument.

---

## Rule 2 — Prefer Academic Sources in the Theoretical Framework

Books and peer-reviewed articles should dominate Chapter 2.

Reports should be used mainly for market context.

---

## Rule 3 — Use Reports Carefully

Reports may contextualize trends, but they should not be used as theoretical foundation.

---

## Rule 4 — Do Not Overclaim Causality

Use methodological references to justify cautious language.

Recommended wording:

```text id="mdm8ap"
The data suggest an association between the redesign and improved funnel performance.
```

Avoid:

```text id="peoynf"
The redesign proved that automation caused sales growth.
```
