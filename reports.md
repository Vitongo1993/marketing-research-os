# Important Note

Reports should be used only as complementary evidence.

They should not replace academic literature.

Academic sources should support the theoretical framework, while reports should support market context, managerial relevance and current business trends.

Before using any report in the final TCC, verify:

* report title
* year
* publisher
* URL
* access date
* methodology
* sample size, when available
* geographic scope
* credibility of the institution
* ABNT formatting

# Report Selection Logic

A report should be included only if it supports at least one of the following arguments:

```text id="phb8ko"
1. Companies are investing in digital transformation and automation.
2. Customer experience has become a strategic performance driver.
3. CRM and customer data are central to commercial management.
4. Speed of response affects customer engagement and conversion.
5. Chatbots and conversational channels are increasingly used in customer service.
6. AI is being used to support marketing, service and sales operations.
7. Healthcare and health-related services require trust and strong customer experience.
8. Marketing and sales teams need better funnel visibility and measurement.
```

# 11. Report Evaluation Checklist

Before using a report, evaluate:

```text id="vsw00z"
1. Is the source credible?
2. Is the report recent enough?
3. Is the methodology clear?
4. Is the sample size disclosed?
5. Is the geographic scope relevant?
6. Is the report commercial or independent?
7. Does it directly support the Telex case?
8. Can the data be cited without overgeneralization?
9. Does the report complement, rather than replace, academic literature?
10. Can it be formatted correctly in ABNT?
```

---

# 12. Reports to Avoid or Use with Caution

Avoid using reports that:

* do not disclose methodology
* are only promotional material
* make exaggerated claims
* have unclear data sources
* are outdated
* are unrelated to Brazil or the case context
* focus on sectors unrelated to services, CRM, marketing or healthcare

Use commercial platform reports with caution because they may have sales or product promotion bias.

# 15. How Reports Should Support the Telex Argument

Reports should help support the following contextual argument:

```text id="azwjir"
The Telex case is relevant because modern customer journeys increasingly depend on fast digital interaction, CRM integration, automation, customer experience quality and operational alignment between marketing and sales.
```

Reports should not be used to prove the Telex results.

The Telex results must come from internal data.

Reports should only help situate those results within broader market trends.
