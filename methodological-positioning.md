# Purpose

This document defines the methodological positioning of the Telex Soluções Auditivas case study.

Its purpose is to explain how the research should be classified, structured and defended academically within the MBA Final Project.

This document connects the business case to scientific research standards and supports the development of the TCC methodology chapter.

---

# Research Context

The Telex Soluções Auditivas case analyzes the redesign of a customer journey and lead management process between 2024 and 2025.

The original model was based on a centralized call center responsible for contacting leads, understanding customer needs, mediating scheduling with stores and confirming appointments.

The redesigned model reduced entry friction, introduced chatbot-based interaction, integrated CRM and automation tools, and transferred lead ownership to the stores.

The study investigates how this operational redesign was associated with changes in:

* lead volume
* appointment scheduling
* attendance
* sales conversion
* response time
* scheduling time
* operational efficiency

---

# Recommended Methodological Classification

The study should be positioned as:

```text
Applied Research
Single Case Study
Documentary Research
Mixed-Method Approach
Before-and-After Analysis
Descriptive and Exploratory Research
```

This combination is appropriate because the project analyzes a real business problem, uses internal operational data, compares two periods and interprets changes through marketing, CRM and customer journey theory.

# Methodological Limitations

This study has important limitations that must be clearly stated.

---

## 1. No Randomized Control Group

The study compares two periods before and after an intervention.

It does not use a randomized experimental design.

Therefore, the findings should be interpreted as evidence-supported associations, not definitive causal proof.

---

## 2. External Variables

The results may have been influenced by external variables such as:

* media investment
* campaign quality
* seasonality
* promotional conditions
* pricing
* sales training
* store-level performance
* product availability
* regional demand

---

## 3. Incomplete CAC Calculation

The operational cost analysis does not represent full Customer Acquisition Cost.

It excludes:

* paid media investment
* benefits
* taxes
* store-level costs
* creative production
* management overhead
* indirect costs

Therefore, the analysis should refer to estimated operational efficiency, not full acquisition cost.

---

## 4. Lead Quality Measurement

The dataset indicates an increase in low-quality or ghost leads, but a more precise analysis would require detailed classification of:

* ghost leads
* cold leads
* disqualified leads
* non-responsive leads
* invalid contacts
* reactivated leads

---

## 5. Store-Level Variation

The study does not fully isolate performance differences between stores.

Future analysis should compare:

* store response time
* attendance rate by store
* sales conversion by store
* CSO performance
* regional differences
* lead quality by location

---

# Appropriate Academic Language

Because the study is not experimental, the TCC should avoid overly strong causal language.

## Avoid

```text
The redesign caused the increase in sales.
```

## Prefer

```text
The data suggest that the redesign was associated with an increase in sales.
```

## Avoid

```text
The chatbot improved conversion.
```

## Prefer

```text
The implementation of chatbot-based entry coincided with improvements in response time and funnel performance.
```

## Avoid

```text
The new model proves that store ownership increases conversion.
```

## Prefer

```text
The case provides evidence that store-level ownership may contribute to improved funnel efficiency.
```

# Recommended Methodology Paragraph for TCC

This research is characterized as applied, descriptive and exploratory, using a single case study strategy with documentary analysis and a mixed-method approach. The study analyzes the redesign of the customer journey and lead management process at Telex Soluções Auditivas, comparing operational and commercial indicators from 2024 and 2025. Quantitative analysis is used to compare funnel indicators such as leads generated, appointments scheduled, attendance, sales and conversion rates. Qualitative analysis is used to interpret changes in the operational flow, CRM structure, call center role, automation process and store-level ownership. Since the study is based on a before-and-after comparison and does not use a randomized control group, the findings are interpreted as evidence-supported associations rather than definitive causal proof.
